# Research (checked 2026-11-21)
- Jurassic: ~201.4 to ~143-145 Ma (Wikipedia "Jurassic"; ICS gives 201.4-145.0). Video says "il y a environ 200 millions d'années" to "145".
- Cretaceous: ~145 to 66 Ma (ICS). End = Cretaceous-Paleogene extinction, ~66 Ma (video: "il y a environ 66 millions d'années"; the title's 65 is the rounded classic figure, flagged as « environ »).
- T. rex: ~69.4-66 Ma, Late Cretaceous, western North America (Wikipedia "Tyrannosaurus"). So not Jurassic.
- Velociraptor: Late Cretaceous ~75-71 Ma, turkey-sized (1.5-2 m long, 14-20 kg); feathers: quill knobs on a forearm (Turner et al. 2007), plus feathered close relatives (Microraptor, Zhenyuanlong) (Wikipedia "Velociraptor").
- Brachiosaurus: Late Jurassic (Morrison Fm, ~154-150 Ma) - standard; the museum mount photos show B. brancai (Tendaguru, Late Jurassic).
- "Jurassic" named after the Jura Mountains (France/Switzerland), coined by Alexandre Brongniart 1829 (Wikipedia "Jurassic").
- Time gap: Brachiosaurus to T. rex ~ 80 million years; T. rex is closer to us than to Stegosaurus.
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic , /Velociraptor , /Tyrannosaurus , stratigraphy.org chart.
